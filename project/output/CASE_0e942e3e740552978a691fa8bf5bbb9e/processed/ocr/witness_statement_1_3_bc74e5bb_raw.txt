indicated STOP for vehicles from his side, and directly collided with the right side of the Activa. The
impact was so severe that the Activa rider was thrown off the scooter and landed on the road about
10-12 feet away. The Pulsar rider also fell down but got up quickly. He tried to pick up his bike to
