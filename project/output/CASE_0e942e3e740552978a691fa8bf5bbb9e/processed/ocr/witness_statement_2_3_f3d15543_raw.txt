Statement Text: My name is Prakash Chandra Joshi. I run a tea stall on Shankar Road for last 22
years. My shop is exactly opposite the gate number 2 of St. Stephen's Hospital. On 15th October
2024, as usual my shop was open. Around 6:15-6:20 PM, I was making tea for some customers. I
heard a loud screeching sound of brakes followed by a big thud sound. I immediately came out of
my shop and saw that two motorcycles/scooters had collided at the T-point just 30-35 feet from my
shop. One person was lying on the road near the divider, another person was getting up. I saw that
a red motorcycle had crashed into a white Activa scooter. The traffic policeman who was on duty
there, I know him as Asif bhai, he immediately ran and caught the red motorcycle rider. The red
motorcycle rider was trying to leave but Asif constable caught his arm and stopped him. I also went
near to help. The person who fell on the road was bleeding from his head and leg. His leg was bent
in wrong direction, I think it was broken. He was crying in pain. I helped the traffic constable to shift
the injured person to one side of the road safely so that no other vehicle hits him. I also noticed that
the red motorcycle rider was wearing a t-shirt of some food company, red color t-shirt. He was also
having a delivery box on his motorcycle. The traffic constable was scolding him for overspeeding
and jumping signal. I have seen many accidents at this T-point over the years because vehicles
come very fast from Shankar Road and don't stop even when traffic signal is not working. The
al-bulance from the hospital came quickly as it is just opposite. Police also came within some time.
I gave water to the injured person before ambulance came. I have also given my name and mobile
number to the police when they came. Whatever I have said is true and I can identify both the
vehicles and the persons involved if called to do so.
