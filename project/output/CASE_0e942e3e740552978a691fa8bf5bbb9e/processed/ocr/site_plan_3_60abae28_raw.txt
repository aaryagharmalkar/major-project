ACCIDENT RECONSTRUCTION POSITIONS
Pre Impact Path Pulsar: Vehicle approaches from SW on Shankar Road, traveling NE in right
lane. Crosses Canara Bank ATM at 60 kmph (CCTV). Approaching junction, does not stop at stop
line. Enters junction at 52-58 kmph.
Pre Impact Path Activa: Vehicle approaches from South on Rajinder Nagar Road. Reaches
T-junction. Indicator on for right turn towards Old Rajinder Nagar. Begins legal right turn at 10
kmph.
Impact Configuration: Pulsar front (traveling NE) collides with Activa right side (turning right).
Impact at 75-80 degree angle. Activa rider thrown to right/east, landing near road edge.
Post Impact Positions: Pulsar falls right side, skids to middle of junction facing East. Activa
displaced right and comes to rest near South-East edge of junction facing South.
